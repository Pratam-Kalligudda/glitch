---
title: "Types and values"
goal: Know exactly what a value is, where it lives and what copying it does.
---
Go's types are few, but each has precise rules about what a value is and what happens when
you assign it, pass it or append to it. This part works through them from zero values to
method sets, with output that shows the rules in action, so that sharing and copying never
surprise you when the later parts add interfaces, generics and goroutines.
